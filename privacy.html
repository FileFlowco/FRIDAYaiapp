Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026.

FileFlow ("we") runs fileflowco.com. This page explains what we collect through the site and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you use the contact form, feedback form or chat assistant, we receive what you type: for example your name, email, phone number, business name and message. Cloudflare Turnstile may process technical signals from your browser to check that you're not a bot. Our host keeps standard server logs such as IP address and request time.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you personally.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, prepare quotes, deliver work you've asked for, and keep the site safe from spam and abuse. We don't sell your information and we don't add you to marketing lists.

AI processing

Chat messages and form submissions are processed by an AI model from Anthropic to answer your questions and to filter out spam. They aren't used by us to train AI models. Chat history is kept only in your browser tab and is cleared when you close it, unless you ask the assistant to send your request to us, in which case the conversation is emailed to FileFlow.

Testimonials

Feedback is published only if you tick the box allowing it, and only with your first name and last initial.

Your choices

Email fileflowinc@gmail.com to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
