Questions

Contact us
How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. One-page websites and website AI assistants start at $499. Everything else is quoted individually after a free consultation, and you always get a fixed price in writing before anything starts.

How long does a project take?

One-page websites and AI assistants usually go live within one to two weeks. Business websites take two to three weeks, AI agents one to three weeks, and full platforms or mobile apps usually six to ten weeks. Your quote includes a real timeline.

How do payments work?

You can pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Zelle or bank transfer. Online payments go through a secure Stripe link. Your quote shows exactly when each payment is due.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Most of our clients aren't. You tell us what's slowing you down, we turn it into something that works and explain it in plain language.

Do you work with small businesses or only large companies?

Both. We build for solo owners, local shops, startups and established companies. The scope changes, the standard doesn't.

Can you work on an existing website or app?

Yes. We fix, update and extend existing projects, and can take over code someone else built.

Who owns the work?

It depends on the project. What you own, and when, is written into your quote before anything starts.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send it over before the first call and we'll sign it.

What happens after launch?

We stay on for fixes, changes and new features. Care plans and AI plans include hosting, monitoring and updates for a monthly fee.

Where are you based?

San Mateo, California. We work with clients across the Bay Area in person and with clients worldwide remotely, in English, Portuguese and Spanish.
