Quick answers.

Does FRIDAY work without internet?

Yes. Once the model is downloaded, FRIDAY works in airplane mode. You only need a connection if you choose to turn on a cloud provider.

Why isn't the model downloading?

Make sure you're on Wi-Fi and the app stays open. The download can take 5 to 15 minutes depending on your connection. If it fails, tap Retry on the download screen.

Is there a subscription?

No. On-device AI has no monthly cost. If you connect a cloud provider, you pay that provider directly. We take nothing.

Which iPhones are supported?

iPhone 12 or newer on iOS 17 or later. The default 3B model works best on iPhone 14 and newer. See Models for details.

My API key isn't working.

Paste the full key, including any prefix like "sk-". Then check that API access and billing are enabled on your provider's dashboard.

How do I delete my data?

Everything lives only on your device. Clear FRIDAY's data in iOS Settings or delete the app. We don't have a copy of anything.
