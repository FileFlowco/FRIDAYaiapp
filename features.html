Real AI.
Zero cloud.

Every feature runs on your iPhone. No internet required, no subscription, and no compromise on privacy.

On-device intelligence

Llama 3.2 runs on Apple's Neural Engine and Metal GPU at 20 to 40 tokens per second. No servers, no latency spikes, no downtime.

Total privacy

Your messages never leave your phone. Every word stays on-device, encrypted by iOS and never logged.

Multi-turn chat

Full conversation context, so FRIDAY follows the whole thread while you work.

Chat history

Every conversation is saved locally. Browse, search and pick up past threads anytime, even offline.

Voice input

Dictate with on-device speech recognition. Transcription happens on your iPhone.

Airplane mode ready

Once the model is downloaded, FRIDAY works anywhere: flights, remote places, no signal at all.

Instant responses

No round trip to a server, no rate limits and no outages. Replies start the moment you hit send.

Rich formatting

Code blocks, lists, headings and bold text render cleanly, so answers are always easy to read.

Optional cloud boost

Need more power for a big task? Connect your own OpenAI or Anthropic key. Switch back to on-device with one tap.

The model runs on your chip. Not theirs.

When you set up FRIDAY, the model is stored directly on your iPhone. Every word of every answer is generated by your device, not a server farm somewhere else.

That means no API limits, no outages and no waiting. Your AI is always available because it's always with you.

Explore models

Bring your own key. Or don't.

For heavy reasoning, long documents or complex code, connect your own API key. Your key goes straight from your phone to the provider, stored in the iOS Keychain. FRIDAY never sees it.

  • On-device · Llama 3.2Default, always included
  • OpenAI · GPT-4oOptional, your own key
  • Anthropic · ClaudeOptional, your own key

Privacy isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

Every decision starts with one question: does this need to send data anywhere? If the answer is yes, it doesn't ship, or it's optional.
